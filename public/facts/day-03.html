Bananas are berries. Strawberries aren't.

On Instagram now

Botany doesn't care about your fruit bowl.

To a botanist, a berry grows from a single flower with one ovary and keeps its seeds inside. Bananas, grapes and even tomatoes qualify. Strawberries don't: the juicy part grows from the base of the flower, and each "seed" on the outside is actually a tiny fruit.
